I am Sanath Thilakarathna, a Mechatronics Engineer and Lecturer based in Sri Lanka. I currently lecture in Mechatronics Engineering at **CINEC Campus** and am reading for an **MSc in Industrial Automation at the University of Moratuwa**, researching bio-inspired polarized skylight sensors for GPS-denied navigation. I hold a **BSc (Hons) in Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering from the University of Ruhuna**.

My technical focus centers on embedded systems, robotics, real-time control, and computer vision—emphasizing hardware–software integration and reliable, deployable engineering design. Alongside teaching and research, I actively develop technical educational content in both Sinhala and English.
