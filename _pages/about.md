I am Sanath Thilakarathna, a Mechatronics Engineer and Lecturer based in Sri Lanka. I currently lecture in Mechatronics Engineering at **CINEC Campus**, teaching Control Engineering, Image Processing & Computer Vision, Microcontroller Engineering, Virtual Instrumentation, and Mechatronics System Design, as well as supervising undergraduate engineering capstone projects.

In parallel with teaching, I am reading for an **MSc in Industrial Automation at the University of Moratuwa**, where my thesis research focuses on developing a bee-inspired polarized skylight sensor for orientation estimation in GPS-denied environments. I hold a **BSc (Hons) in Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering from the University of Ruhuna**, where my final-year engineering research focused on real-time parameter monitoring and optical viscosity sensing for industrial manufacturing lines.

My background connects academic research with practical industrial engineering, drawing on experience from **ALDTAN (Pvt) Ltd** (managing engineering projects and custom National Instruments DAQ software), **LE Robotics** (low-level firmware for SCARA robot end-effectors and kinematic modeling), and **Dilmah Ceylon Tea Company** (factory utilities and industrial robotics).

My core technical competencies and research interests include:
- **Robotics & Autonomous Systems:** Forward/inverse kinematics, planar parallel mechanisms, mobile robotics, and bio-inspired navigation sensors.
- **Embedded Systems & Microcontrollers:** Register-level architecture (ATmega, ESP32 with ESP-IDF, STM32), real-time sensor–actuator interfacing, and deterministic DAQ.
- **Computer Vision & Control:** Edge computer vision, camera calibration, optical tracking, and robust feedback control.

Alongside teaching and engineering research, I actively publish technical articles and educational materials in Sinhala and English, aiming to bridge theory, hardware design, and practical industry deployment.
